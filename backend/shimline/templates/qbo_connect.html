Connecting QuickBooks is set up by your bookkeeper.

Shimline does not ask clients to manage their own integration. Your bookkeeper starts the connection from inside Shimline and sends you the approval link, so a connection is never created by someone landing on a page.

When you approve it, Shimline receives accounting access. We inspect the books and can apply a correction only after it is evidenced and explicitly approved; we never delete entries or silently overwrite them. You can end access at any time from Settings → Apps in QuickBooks.
